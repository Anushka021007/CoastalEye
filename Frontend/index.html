How It Works

1. Detect

Deep learning U-Net model analyzes Sentinel-1 satellite radar imagery to identify oil spills across coastal waters.

2. Correlate

AIS ship tracking data reveals which vessels were near the spill, including dark ship events where transponders were disabled.

3. Attribute

Ranked suspect vessels with evidence scores, ready for coast guard enforcement action.
